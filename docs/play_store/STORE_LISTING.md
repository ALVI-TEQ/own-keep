# OwnKeep Google Play Listing Draft

## App name

OwnKeep

## Short description

Keep important documents organized in a private, encrypted local vault.

## Full description

OwnKeep helps you organize important personal documents without sending your vault to an OwnKeep server.

Key capabilities:

- encrypted local document storage;
- PIN and device-biometric protection;
- document import, scanning, preview and local OCR;
- search, tags, favourites, reminders and custom collections;
- on-device document insights and organization;
- encrypted portable backup, complete restore and file-based device migration;
- local integrity and security checks.

Your vault remains under your control. The current Android release has no OwnKeep account, advertisements, analytics or production Internet permission. Exports occur only after you choose a destination through Android.

Some concepts shown in project design material—cloud family sync, hidden/decoy vaults, nearby transfer, PDF merge/split and selective restore—are not included in this release and must not appear in Play screenshots or marketing.
