# OwnKeep Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 August 2026

OwnKeep is a local-first encrypted document vault. The current release does not create an online account, operate an OwnKeep cloud service, include advertising, or include analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.

## Data handled by the app

OwnKeep can process documents, photos, document metadata, reminders, tags, locally generated OCR text, and security preferences selected by the user. This information is processed on the user's device and stored in the app's encrypted local vault.

OwnKeep does not transmit vault content to the developer. When a user explicitly exports an encrypted backup or document, Android's system document picker transfers the selected output to a destination chosen by the user. The destination provider's terms and privacy policy apply after that choice.

## Device capabilities

OwnKeep may use system-provided document/photo pickers, document scanning, notifications, and biometric authentication when the user starts the corresponding feature. Biometric templates are managed by Android and are not available to OwnKeep.

The production Android manifest does not request Internet access. Development and profiling builds use Internet access only for Flutter development tooling.

## Data retention and deletion

Vault data remains on the device until the user deletes individual records, empties deleted items, clears the app's storage, uninstalls the app, or uses OwnKeep's wipe-vault control. Exported files and backups must be deleted separately from the destination selected by the user.

OwnKeep does not create an online account, so there is no server-side account or server-held personal data to delete.

## Children

OwnKeep is a general productivity application and is not directed to children.

## Changes

This policy will be updated before adding any network service, analytics, advertising, account system, subscription verification service, or other off-device data processing.

## Contact

Privacy questions may be sent to privacy@alviteq.com. Do not include vault contents, passwords, PINs, recovery phrases, identity documents, or other sensitive records.
